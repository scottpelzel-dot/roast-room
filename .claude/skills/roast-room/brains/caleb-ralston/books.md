# Books: Caleb Ralston

Built from the four notes batches on all 8 sources (S001 to S008). He has no published books; his teaching lives in long free YouTube courses, listed below with the outside books he cites. Summaries are in my own words.

## His major courses and series

### How to Build a Personal Brand (Full Course) (S005)
- **Date:** Undated. It references February 2025, and he calls it only his second YouTube video. It is likely the free 6 hour 22 minute course he says he released on April 4, 2025 (inferred from S004, S005).
- **Core thesis:** Brand is an intentional pairing done consistently, and educational content exists to scale trust, which is what makes selling easy.
- **Key frameworks:** brand as pairing; Brand Journey Framework; catalyst, core truth, proof as the brand story; 80/20 content focus; accordion method; Eye of Sauron; waterfall distribution; multiplier sheet; 70/20/10; content hackathons; platform-owner team (85/15); hiring funnel, tryouts and the four Rs job description; 30/60/90 onboarding; share the knowledge, sell the execution; offer stack.
- **Result he reports:** 22,000 email subscribers from zero and over 500 applications in five months (S004); later 1.3 million views and over 45,000 leads (S002).

### How to Scale Your Personal Brand to 10M Followers (S004)
- **Date:** Undated; he says he is speaking in the back half of 2025.
- **Core thesis:** A personal brand moves through four stages (foundation, trust, lead the space, media company), and the system that got you to one stage will not get you to the next.
- **Key frameworks:** the four stages; three levers (contrarian belief, delivery, wrapping paper); deep, niche-wide and personal ratios by stage; primary and secondary platforms with graduation rules; outlier multipliers and the experiment kill rule; metrics by stage; team build order; the stage 3 studio model; bar stool method.

### How to START a Personal Brand (Full Course) (S007)
- **Date:** Undated; recorded after his first year of posting (inferred from the year-one numbers in S007).
- **Core thesis:** Decide whether you want followers or customers, then build for trust with a system that fits your life.
- **Key frameworks:** Brand Journey Framework; credibility bank; two-column approach and brand statement; three levers; gift formula with contextual credibility; wrapping paper library; 75/20/5; Four C's intro; first three videos. The last third is a podcast where his content director is mislabeled with the same speaker tag, so only lines clearly his are used.

## Outside books and works he cites

### Crush It (Gary Vaynerchuk)
- **Why it matters to him:** A friend gave it to him at 15 and he says it was the only book he read in high school. It convinced him a full-time living could come from video content and is why he cites Gary so often.
- **Cited in:** S001, S004, S005.

### 86 Pages of Insanity (Gary Vaynerchuk)
- **Why it matters to him:** He calls it a project, not strictly a book, from around 2017 or 2018, and the source of the content pyramid that his waterfall distribution method adapts.
- **Cited in:** S004.

### Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule (Paul Graham, an essay)
- **Why it matters to him:** He runs maker time in the morning and manager time in the afternoon, with one full maker day a week for his team.
- **Cited in:** S005.
