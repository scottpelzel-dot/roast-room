# Principles: Kane Kallaway

Built from extract notes on 33 of his own YouTube videos (S001, S003 to S034; S002 is excluded as a translated transcript). 30 decision rules, most-supported first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. All sources are undated except S020, a 2025 video by its title. Sponsor reads (S006) and pitches for his own Sandcastles software are not counted as positions.

1. **Use contrast to create curiosity: state what they believe, then break it.** "Contrast is the single most reliable way to create curiosity in content." (S015) "All curiosity is rooted in a state change from A to B." (S016) Sources: 14 (S001, S003, S008, S012, S013, S014, S015, S016, S018, S022, S024, S026, S032, S033). Confidence: high.

2. **The hook decides the video; fix it before anything else.** "if the first line doesn't immediately grab the viewer in short form video You're Dead" (S001) "Hooks are the 80 of the 8020 in the content flow." (S014) Sources: 10 (S001, S008, S014, S016, S021, S022, S023, S026, S030, S032). Confidence: high.

3. **Clarity beats cleverness; people leave because they are confused, not because they dislike you.** "Simple words, no clever word play, no vague setups." (S023) "People do not feel good when they feel dumb." (S034) Sources: 10 (S005, S011, S014, S017, S023, S024, S031, S032, S034, S016). Confidence: high.

4. **Signal value in the first seconds and cut everything before the topic.** "You want to increase the speed that the viewer can get to clarity or get to value." (S014) "In short-form land, you've got about 2.5 seconds to signal value." (S030) Sources: 8 (S008, S014, S024, S026, S030, S031, S032, S034). Confidence: high. The window he names ranges from one to five seconds across sources.

5. **Pick data over gut: study what already won, then remix it.** "Using data makes it much easier to win faster, because humans cannot outrun their psychology." (S025) "The best predictor of future success for hooks on your channel is to study the other hooks that have already won on your channel." (S011) Sources: 9 (S009, S011, S012, S019, S021, S022, S023, S025, S027). Confidence: high.

6. **One channel, one topic, one buyer; say no to viral ideas for the wrong audience.** "And this means sometimes saying no to ideas that seem viral, but would resonate with the wrong audience." (S027) "What works is that you pick one topic and hammer it over and over and over and over." (S020) Sources: 7 (S012, S013, S019, S020, S027, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

7. **Re-hook on a clock; never leave a gap between one loop closing and the next opening.** "There should never be a gap." (S018) "After every major section or key point in your script, add a loop opener that smoothly transitions the viewer to what's next and resets their attention clock." (S015) Sources: 7 (S003, S015, S018, S024, S031, S033, S034). Confidence: high.

8. **Get the proof first, then make the content; a claim without proof gets dismissed.** "The fix is to first get the proof, then make the content based on the proof, not the other way around." (S004) "Given the current state of the internet with all these fake gurus, making a strong claim in the hook is not enough to get someone to lock in." (S023) Sources: 7 (S004, S013, S016, S022, S023, S024, S026). Confidence: high.

9. **Be non-obvious: say something new, or say the old thing in a new way.** "Non-obvious ideas create obsessive audiences. Tattoo that to your head." (S004) "You either say something people haven't heard before, that's new information, or you say something they have heard before, but you say it in a new way, that's new delivery." (S030) Sources: 7 (S004, S005, S012, S013, S019, S030, S033). Confidence: high.

10. **A business plays the conversion game: judge content by leads and sales, not views.** "Now, the second uncommon lesson that I've learned about social media growth is that virality is a trap." (S029) "But only a few of them are really good at driving views that actually end up converting into leads and sales." (S007) Sources: 6 (S007, S012, S016, S024, S027, S029). Confidence: high.

11. **Bait the click, then pay it off at once; a promise you do not keep is fraud.** "You 100% want to bait the click. That is your job with the thumbnail." (S010) "you don't actually have anything at the end of the rainbow people would just churn and you're a fraud" (S008) Sources: 5 (S008, S010, S013, S023, S033). Confidence: high.

12. **The idea is the bottleneck: if it is weak, nothing downstream saves it.** "Nothing else matters if the idea is weak." (S025) "if I don't have unique points in the outline I'm not moving forward I'm not making the video" (S033) Sources: 6 (S012, S025, S026, S032, S033, S013). Confidence: high.

13. **Make the visual, spoken and text hooks say one thing.** "The visual, spoken, and text hook must be aligned." (S026) "They work on the spoken hook, but they don't align all three hooks and that's why their videos fail." (S023) Sources: 5 (S011, S023, S026, S032, S001). Confidence: high.

14. **Stop the scroll visually in the first one to two seconds, with your own look rather than a copied one.** "Think of stimulation like a visual stun gun that gets someone to stop and lock in on one thing." (S003) "But the reality is, social media is a visual slot machine." (S030) Sources: 6 (S003, S008, S011, S030, S032, S010). Confidence: high. He ranks text hooks above visuals in S023, so the weight given to visuals varies by video.

15. **Talk to one person, about their need, using "you" not "I".** "say the word you or your instead of the words me or I when you're delivering the hook." (S014) "Learning about trends in skincare is a nice to have. Solving my acne problem is a need to have." (S014) Sources: 6 (S001, S013, S014, S024, S007, S004). Confidence: high.

16. **Volume while you learn, but never below a quality floor.** "Do not dip below 7 out of 10 quality cuz as we've mentioned, anything below that will fail immediately." (S021) "Violent volume without sacrificing quality." (S019) Sources: 5 (S011, S013, S019, S021, S030). Confidence: high. He says he has flip-flopped on quantity versus quality; his latest framing is a shift to quality first because supply is now infinite (S019, S021).

17. **Win one home platform before spreading out; email is the one cross-platform link that works.** "One platform means one audience, one algorithm, one skill set compounding, always active until you dominate it." (S019) "Now, I will say the only cross-platform content medium that does work well to share your YouTube links is email." (S013) Sources: 3 (S013, S019, S029). Confidence: high.

18. **Own a narrow category instead of chasing broad fame.** "you're almost better off being first in a niche category than being third, fourth, or fifth in the overall ranking in someone's mind." (S005) "The riches are in the niches and the niches are best activated with ontarget virality." (S029) Sources: 4 (S005, S019, S029, S013). Confidence: high.

19. **Delete tangents: every line must be needed context or a step in the logic.** "Your job as a script writer or storyteller is not to use every word or fact at your disposal." (S034) "I don't care that you were at a conference; just tell me what the CEO said." (S031) Sources: 4 (S014, S031, S034, S026). Confidence: high.

20. **Name your best ideas, but not everything.** "People can relate to a single word name more than a long definition." (S034) "Not everything needs to be term branded, so don't go too crazy." (S015) Sources: 4 (S015, S016, S026, S034). Confidence: high.

21. **Test options and let the market pick, instead of trusting taste.** "you really should be testing with at least three thumbnails." (S010) "Every post on this R&D account is intentional and a test." (S028) Sources: 4 (S010, S021, S028, S030). Confidence: high.

22. **Remix, do not copy-paste; psychology does not travel across niches.** "You're not copy-pasting what's already worked. That's corny and lame." (S023) "When you do these crazy copy-paste jobs from one niche all the way over to the other, they usually don't work either, because the psychology doesn't translate." (S009) Sources: 4 (S009, S012, S022, S023). Confidence: high.

23. **Do not play a character; obsession with the topic is the one thing AI cannot clone.** "people can detect when you're playing a character" (S024) "But the one input AI cannot clone is a genuine obsession in the topic." (S019) Sources: 4 (S003, S005, S019, S024). Confidence: high.

24. **Give the answers away; sell speed to the people who want it done faster.** "everything I do is optimize to build trust that's why I give the answers away" (S024) "it's the other 95% that want to pay for Speed" (S024) Sources: 4 (S004, S008, S024, S034). Confidence: high.

25. **Use attention to feed owned offers, not ad revenue.** "It's better to use media to gain attention and then funnel that media to owned products and services than it is to rely on the value capture AdSense and brand deals from the media." (S029) "This is not an AdSense play. That's not why you're doing it." (S013) Sources: 3 (S013, S020, S029). Confidence: high.

26. **Use AI for research and cleanup, fed with real data; do not let it write the whole script.** "I'll be honest, I personally don't really like an AI program writing my scripts end to end." (S026) "When you write hooks with AI, the LLMs just aren't optimized for short-form social data." (S009) Sources: 4 (S009, S014, S026, S034). Confidence: high.

27. **Speak with certainty; hedging costs attention and comments.** "Close the doubt, stop being wishy-washy and tell them the truth bluntly like it is." (S015) "If you play the middle and hedge, you're going to get fewer comments." (S027) Sources: 2 (S015, S027). Confidence: medium.

28. **Ignore algorithm folklore; posting time, hashtags and caption tricks do not move reach.** "Posting time does not matter, hashtags in your captions don't matter." (S027) "Posting at the same time has nothing to do with the algorithm, it's more just to help keep you accountable so you have a schedule that you don't miss." (S030) Sources: 2 (S027, S030). Confidence: medium. Conflict: S029 says "Most algorithms run their audience match targeting based on the video transcription and the caption." (S029), so caption content may matter for targeting even if caption tricks do not.

29. **Deliver with more energy than feels natural.** "You need to give 50% more energy than you think you need, and this will compensate for the voice confrontation problem." (S017) Sources: 2 (S017, S024). Confidence: medium.

30. **Build real skill before you teach it on camera.** "shouldn't be building a personal brand around some topic unless you have a deep foundational knowledge set of that topic." (S005) "For example, if I make videos on content growth, but I've never actually grown with content before, well, then what good is my take?" (S013) Sources: 2 (S005, S013). Confidence: medium.
