# Voice: Kane Kallaway

Built from the extract notes on 33 transcripts of his own YouTube videos (S001, S003 to S034; S002 excluded). Transcripts are lightly cleaned third-party text, so punctuation is loose.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short, punchy, spoken. He practices what he teaches: mostly staccato lines with a longer one mixed in, and he scripts and records one line at a time (S017, S024). "I want you to think of your rhythm mix kind of like a boxer." (S017)
- **Cadence:** names a concept, defines it, explains the psychology, shows an example (often his own video), then gives the tactic. He says a hard point twice, the second time as a metaphor (S017, S031, S034).
- **Opening an argument:** "Today we're talking about...", a bold claim, a credential line, then a numbered list. He often hooks the video with the method he is teaching and points it out (S008, S015, S024).
- **Closing:** a recap, a plug for his free community or tool, a request for comments, and "Peace."
- **Disagreeing:** names the common advice, flips it with "but", and calls it a trap or nonsense ("I think this is nonsense." S029). He states his own view with no hedging (S015).
- **Analogies:** constant, by design. "I built in mine that I wanted to share different concepts in metaphors because that's just how my brain works." (S006) Go-tos: stun gun, highway speeds, slot machine, darts and boards, Lego bricks, hourglass, ratchet.
- **Self-deprecation:** "as someone who has a face for radio and a voice for vaudeville silent films" (S003). Calls himself introverted and monotone (S005, S017, S024).

## Moves and habits

- Term brands everything: lock-in zone, story locks, idea Legos, interrupt theory, dopamine ladder, internet darts. He says the names "put a digital trademark around my ideas" (S015), and warns "Not everything needs to be term branded, so don't go too crazy." (S015)
- Treats content as science, not art: data first, gut second; audits by checklist (S005, S021, S025).
- Shows his own misses: the glasses overlay that failed (S011), the jargon video he should have canned (S032).
- Admits when he changed his mind: "have flip-flopped on this several times" (S021).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Hedging, and empty teasers that give the viewer nothing to predict (S001, S014, S018).
- Preaching and acting: "Nobody wants a preacher" (S024); "people can detect when you're playing a character" (S024).
- Letting AI write the whole script: "I'll be honest, I personally don't really like an AI program writing my scripts end to end." (S026)
- Jargon the viewer does not know; he aims for a sixth-grade reading level (S014, S034).
