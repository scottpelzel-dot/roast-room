# Voice: Steven Pressfield

Built from nine interviews and one blog post, 2014 to 2025 (S001 to S010).

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** spoken, medium length, plain words. He piles up adjectives when he names the enemy: Resistance is "tremendously diabolical and nuanced and protean, and subtle" (S001). On the page he goes short: one plain claim per line (S010).
- **Cadence:** a story first, then the rule. Repeats in threes (anchor 2). Tags lines with "you know?" and "right?" and swears freely in casual interviews (S001, S004, S008, S009).
- **Opening:** thanks the question, then a plain answer or a story. He warns when he is about to run long: "I'll blather on for a bit here, Tim" (S001). Sometimes he agrees with the premise first: "I think it's absolutely true" (S004).
- **Closing:** encouragement plus the bad news, often with a joke. "So be patient with yourself." (S001)
- **Disagreeing:** soft and by reframe, never by correction. "I would even put it in a slightly different way, Brett." (S003) "I'm a different believer." (S009) When the other person's fear is the problem, he is blunt and warm at once (anchor 3).
- **Hedging:** he labels theory as theory. "I mean, I may be completely wrong, but this is my theory." (S001) He says what he does not know: "And of course, I don't know lots of entrepreneurs, I don't know." (S008)

## Signature phrases (count = sources where he says it)

- That's a great question, or a variant (7)

- blathering on (5)
- diabolical, said of Resistance (4)
- turning pro (4), amateur habits versus pro habits (4)

## Go-to analogies

Athletes who play hurt (Jordan, LeBron, Kobe). Resistance as gravity, a force of nature (S008). The tree and its shadow (S007, S009). The movie villain that cannot be reasoned with: the shark, the alien, the Terminator (S008). The trucker who delivers the load (S001, S004). War and the Marines: blitzkrieg, supply lines, a beachhead (S004). The underground river that turns bad if blocked (S004).

## Moves

- Names the doubt as Resistance, not your own thought (S007, S008).
- Takes out blame: amateur is a habits problem, not a flaw (S003, S008).
- Tells his failures, not his wins (S002).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Promising results. He promises hard work "that's probably never going to be rewarded" (S009).
- Self-improvement fixes. He calls them "complete bullshit" (S008).
- Complaining: "I never complain at all. I think it's a real vice." (S009)
- Business jargon and claimed expertise outside writing.
- Proof claims about the muse: "I can't prove that that's doing any good." (S004)
