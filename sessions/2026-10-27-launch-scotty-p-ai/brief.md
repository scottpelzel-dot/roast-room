# Brief: how to launch Scotty P.AI on Instagram and TikTok

Mode: full council, six seats (recommended board, confirmed by the founder), three rounds.

## THE BRIEF

**Decision:** How to launch Scotty P.AI, a new AI education brand, and what content to make so it stands out. Options:
- (A) **Broad AI educator.** New Instagram and TikTok accounts posting general AI tips, tools, and tutorials for a wide audience.
- (B) **Narrow build-in-public.** New accounts that document real AI builds for small businesses (starting with the voice agent client), from the angle of a jujutsu coach who builds AI. Aimed at small local business owners; the call to action is "I'll build this for you."
- (C) **Smaller version: borrow the existing audience.** Do not start new accounts yet. Post AI content on the existing Scotty P jujutsu pages, aimed first at gym and coaching business owners, and spin out new accounts only once a few posts and clients prove it.
- (D) **Don't launch content yet.** Spend the hours on direct outreach to land the first 3 to 5 paying AI clients, and start content later.

**The business:** The founder runs an established Scotty P jujutsu coaching brand with existing social pages. Scotty P.AI is new: an AI education brand. The founder has strong content experience. First AI client (setting up an AI voice agent) is tomorrow. No paying AI customers yet, so no AI revenue. Revenue and margin of the jujutsu brand: not given. Team: solo (assumed).

**The resources:** 5 to 10 hours a week for content. Existing supporting pages that can cross-promote and lend credibility. Cash for AI tools and editing: modest (assumed). No hard deadline.

**What success looks like:** Steady inbound leads (people asking to buy) within 6 months. The brand is "AI education", but the goal the founder picked is leads, so the council should say what those leads buy (services, a course, or both).

**Founder's lean:** Start new Instagram and TikTok accounts and go (closest to A or B). He is asking what content to make and how to stand out in a crowded AI niche.

**Assumptions:** Solo founder (assumed). The jujutsu pages have a meaningful following (assumed; size not given). The voice agent client is a small business and is willing to be filmed or named (assumed). AI-tips content on TikTok and Instagram is crowded in 2026 (assumed).
