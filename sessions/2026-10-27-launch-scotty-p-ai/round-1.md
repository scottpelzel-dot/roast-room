SEAT: HORMOZI (Offers, pricing, and sales)

THE CALL: Run D aimed at gym and coaching owners, sell one productized voice agent, and post each build on the existing jujutsu pages (C) as proof, not as the channel.

WHY:
1. You don't have a leads problem yet. You have zero customers, so you have an offer problem. Content with nothing proven to sell is aerodynamics on a car whose engine won't start. Find the constraint first and fix that (Constraint first, S113). Fix the offer first (S114).
2. The math, using my assumptions: $1,500 setup plus $300 a month, about $40 a month in tools. Warm outreach to 20 gym owners a day for 5 weeks is 500 touches. If 4% book a call, that's 20 calls. At a 30% close, that's 6 clients: $9,000 up front and $1,800 a month. CAC is your time, so 30-day cash clears 2x (CAC + COGS) easily (Client-financed acquisition, S037). Option A has a broad audience, no offer and no 30-day cash.
3. Value Equation (S115): the dream outcome is "every missed call books a trial class." For likelihood, film tomorrow's client's real call logs. Time delay: live in 7 days. Effort: done for you. Guarantee: if it doesn't book 10 trials in 30 days, I keep working free (Guarantees, S115). That's the offer.
4. What content and how to stand out: a narrow buyer with a broad persona (S093). Every post follows proof, promise, plan (S041). Example: "This gym missed 40 calls last month. Here's the agent I built that answered them." Keep it 70% give, 30% ask (S052). "likes ain't cash, views ain't cash, cash is cash." (S041)

THE RISK I SEE: "SMBs suck as customers." (S028) Small gyms churn monthly retainers. Then you're on a treadmill of $1,500 setups.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: If 100 warm gym-owner touches book fewer than 2 calls, the offer or the avatar is wrong. Then B's broader local-business angle earns a test.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Film tomorrow's install, then DM 100 gym owners you already know with the clip and one line: "Want this for your gym? Live in 7 days or I keep working free."

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: What does one missed call cost a gym owner in lifetime membership dollars? That number sets your price.
