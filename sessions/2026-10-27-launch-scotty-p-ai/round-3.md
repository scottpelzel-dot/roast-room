Six votes, all "Endorse with amendment". No dissent. Every amendment adopted; none was objected to by another seat.

4. **Buffett, running costs (adopted).** Log per-client running costs from day one; over $100 of the $300 means reprice before client two.
5. **Sara, love test (adopted).** By Dec 31, at least 1 of the first 3 gyms refers another owner unasked or films a 30-second testimonial.
