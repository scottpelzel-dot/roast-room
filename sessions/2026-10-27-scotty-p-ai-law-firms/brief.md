# Brief: Scotty P.AI for lawyers and high-ticket businesses

Mode: quick council, four seats (Hormozi, Gary V, Naval, Buffett), Rounds 1 and 2, Round 3 only if scores do not converge. This reruns an earlier council (sessions/2026-10-27-launch-scotty-p-ai/) with new facts. That council picked gym owners because the brief described the founder as a coaching business owner and did not say who the first client was. The founder has rejected that niche.

## THE BRIEF

**Decision:** Who Scotty P.AI sells AI agents to, how to price them, and how content and the founder's coaching network fit in. Options:
- (A) **Law firms only.** Productized AI intake agent for family, divorce, and criminal law firms (answers missed and after-hours calls, qualifies, books consults, ties into social leads). Content and outreach aimed only at lawyers.
- (B) **High-ticket service businesses broadly.** The same agent sold to any business with high-ticket sales (law firms, plus others such as med spas, contractors, financial advisors). Content aimed at high-ticket owners in general.
- (C) **Prove it on the first lawyer first.** No niche marketing yet. Run the agent for the current lawyer for 60 to 90 days, measure booked consults and retained clients, then pick the niche and price from those numbers. Content is the build-in-public story of this one client.
- (D) **Keep the earlier verdict.** Gym and coaching owners, $1,500 setup plus $300 a month, outreach through the coaching network.

**The business:** The founder runs an established Scotty P jujutsu coaching brand with existing social pages and a strong coaching network. He has content experience and already runs social media management for a lawyer (family, divorce, criminal) at $1,500 a month. That lawyer is the first AI agent client, starting tomorrow. The agent will answer missed and after-hours calls, qualify callers, book consults, and handle leads from the social content the founder already runs for him. No other AI revenue. Solo (assumed).

**The resources:** 5 to 10 hours a week for content. Modest cash for tools (assumed). Coaching network the founder wants to use for creator authority and credibility, not as buyers.

**What success looks like:** Steady inbound leads from high-ticket businesses within 6 months, at prices well above gym-level.

**Pricing on the table:** The founder plans at least $500 a month for the agent on top of the $1,500 social retainer ($2,000 a month total), and hopes to charge more once he can show the agent makes the lawyer money.

**Founder's lean:** Lawyers and high-ticket businesses (A or B). He does not want to sell to coaches: "most of them are struggling." He wants the coaching network to build his authority as a creator.

**Assumptions:** One retained family, divorce, or criminal client is worth about $3,000 to $10,000 in fees to the firm (assumed). Callers in these practice areas are often urgent and call around, so the first firm to answer often wins (assumed). The agent must not give legal advice, and call recording and data rules apply (assumed). The lawyer has agreed to be the case study (assumed, not confirmed).
