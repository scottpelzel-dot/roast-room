# Round 1: opening positions

## Hormozi (weight 3)

SEAT: HORMOZI (Offers, pricing, and sales)
STANCE: Strong yes
OPTION SCORES: A 8/10 · B 4/10 · C 6/10 · D 2/10
PILLAR SCORE: 8/10
CONFIDENCE: 4/5
RUIN RISK: possible
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: A, with C running inside it. Sell only to family, divorce, and criminal firms, and use the first lawyer's 90-day numbers as the proof that sets the price.

WHY:
1. Price is a math problem, and $500 is the wrong answer (Value Equation, S115). My assumption: the agent saves 2 retained clients a month at $5,000 each. That's $10,000 a month for the firm, so $500 is 5% of the value created. Speed and after-hours coverage cut time delay to zero, and the urgent caller who phones around is the dream buyer. Standard offer: $3,000 setup plus $1,500 a month. Guarantee: if the agent doesn't book X consults in 60 days, I keep working free until it does (Guarantees, S115). With the social retainer, that's $3,000 a month per firm.
2. Unit economics clear the bar. Assume $200 a month in tools: $1,300 gross profit a month, 12-month life, about $18,600 lifetime gross profit with setup. Fully loaded CAC of $3,000 gives about 6:1, which is the floor when a person staffs the steps (LTV:CAC, S089). 30-day cash: $4,500 collected against 2 x ($1,000 CAC + $200 COGS) = $2,400 if CAC stays near $1,000 (S037). Customers fund the next customer.
3. B breaks the 111 rule (S059). "Most businesses die from indigestion, not starvation." (S113). Med spas, contractors, and advisors each need their own scripts, proof, and content. One avatar means lower CAC and messaging that lands (S094). D fails the starving-crowd test: no purchasing power, and you already said no to it.
4. First lawyer: "I never discount but I will change terms." (S046). He stays at $500 for 90 days in exchange for the case study, a recorded testimonial, and access to his numbers. Day 91 he moves to standard price.

THE RISK I SEE: the agent gives something that sounds like legal advice, or fumbles a recording-consent rule, and you lose the $1,500 retainer and your only proof together.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: if after 90 days the agent adds fewer than 1 retained client a month, the value isn't there at $1,500 and the offer needs rework.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: call 20 local family and criminal firms after hours, record what happens, and turn it into a "missed-call audit" lead magnet you send to each firm.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: How many calls does your lawyer miss a month right now, and what share of his consults turn into retained clients?
