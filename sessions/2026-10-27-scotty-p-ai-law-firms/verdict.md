## Why the council landed here

- **$500 is far below the value.** If one retained client is worth $3,000 to $10,000 and the agent saves even one a month, $500 is 5% to 15% of what it makes the firm. Get the number, then charge for it. (Hormozi, Value Equation; Naval, price discrimination; Buffett)
- **One niche, not every high-ticket business.** Med spas, contractors, and advisors each need their own scripts, compliance, proof, and content. With 5 to 10 hours a week, that turns you into an agency. (Hormozi, "indigestion, not starvation"; Naval, specific knowledge)
- **The missed-call audit sells the problem before you have results.** Call 20 local family and criminal firms after hours and record who picks up. That is a fact about them, not a claim about you. (Hormozi; Naval and Buffett conceded it)
- **Document, don't claim.** Setup day, the script review, the weekly "what the agent caught" log. LinkedIn is where lawyers pay attention, and it is underpriced. Your jujutsu network is authority: discipline, pressure, the guy who answers when it's urgent. (Gary V)
- **Your reputation is the asset at risk.** One bad call on a criminal line costs the $18,000-a-year retainer and your only reference. (Buffett, "We can afford to lose money but we can't afford to lose reputation.")
